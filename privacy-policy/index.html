1. Introduction

Welcome to VedVaani, an AI-powered astrology application that integrates the wisdom of Vedic astrology with advanced artificial intelligence to provide personalized insights and guidance.

At VedVaani, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines the information we collect, the reasons for its collection, how we use and store it, and the choices you have regarding your data.

By using VedVaani, you acknowledge and agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, we recommend that you discontinue the use of our services.

2. Information We Collect

To provide accurate and personalized astrological guidance, VedVaani collects specific types of personal and non-personal information.

a. Personal Information

When using our app, you may be required to provide the following details:

  • Name (to personalize reports and interactions)
  • Date of Birth, Time of Birth, and Place of Birth (essential for generating accurate astrological insights)
  • Gender (used for horoscope personalization)
  • Email Address (optional, for account-related communication)
  • Profile Information (if users create multiple profiles for different individuals)

This information is voluntarily provided by users to receive personalized astrology readings and reports.

b. Non-Personal Information

VedVaani may automatically collect certain non-personal data to improve user experience and optimize app performance. This includes:

  • Device Information (such as device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers)
  • App Usage Data (such as features accessed, interaction logs, and session duration)
  • IP Address and Location Data (only if permission is granted by the user)
  • Analytics Data (collected through third-party tools such as Google Analytics and Firebase to enhance service quality)

Why We Collect This Data

The information collected serves several purposes, including:

  • To generate personalized astrological insights based on your birth details.
  • To improve the app’s features by analyzing user behavior and preferences.
  • To provide customer support and address inquiries efficiently.
  • To ensure secure and smooth operation of the application.

We strictly do not collect any financial or payment-related information unless explicitly stated for specific services that require transactions.

3. How We Use Your Data

The information collected through VedVaani is used solely for providing and enhancing astrological services.

  • Generating Astrological Reports: Your birth details are processed to create personalized astrology reports, including horoscopes, compatibility analyses, and predictions.
  • Enhancing User Experience: Non-personal data helps improve app functionality, identify potential issues, and ensure a seamless user experience.
  • Customer Support and Communication: If you contact our support team, we may use your details to assist with inquiries and troubleshooting.
  • Security and Fraud Prevention: Data is used to ensure account security, prevent unauthorized access, and safeguard against potential threats.

Data Sharing and Security Measures

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties under any circumstances.
  • Data sharing is strictly limited to essential third-party service providers that help improve app functionality.
  • When data is shared with trusted partners (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase), it is done in compliance with strict privacy and security protocols.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

VedVaani retains user data only for as long as necessary to provide its services or as required by law.

Data Deletion Requests: Users can request the deletion of their data through:

  • The in-app “Delete Account” option.
  • Contact our support team at support@vedvaani.com.

Processing Time: Requests for data deletion will be processed within six months of submission.

If a user deletes their account, all associated personal data will be removed from our active databases, though backups and logs may be retained for security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.

5. Security Measures

We take data security seriously and implement strict measures to protect user information.

  • End-to-End Encryption: Data is encrypted during transmission and storage to prevent unauthorized access.
  • Secure Storage: All personal data is stored in protected databases with restricted access.
  • Regular Security Audits: We conduct periodic evaluations to ensure compliance with industry security standards.
  • Access Control: Only authorized personnel have access to personal data, ensuring privacy and security at all levels.

6. User Rights and Choices

VedVaani respects user rights regarding data privacy and allows users to:

  • Access and Review Data: Users can view the information stored in their profiles.
  • Modify or Update Information: Users can edit their birth details and profile preferences within the app.
  • Request Data Deletion: Users can request complete removal of their data as outlined in Section 4.
  • Opt-Out of Marketing Communications: Users can unsubscribe from promotional emails and notifications via app settings.

7. Third-Party Services

VedVaani may use third-party tools and services to enhance functionality. These include:

Each third-party provider has its own privacy policies, which we encourage users to review. VedVaani ensures that all integrations comply with applicable privacy laws.

VedVaani complies with the privacy and data protection guidelines set forth by the Google Play Store and Apple App Store. We adhere to industry best practices to ensure user data is handled securely and transparently.
